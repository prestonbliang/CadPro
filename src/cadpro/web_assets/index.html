      CAPTURE GUIDE / 10

      Good source views are
      the real model quality.

      1. 01

        Keep the whole object visibleLeave margin around every edge in every view.

      2. 02

        Use soft, even lightAvoid hard moving shadows, glare, and exposure changes.

      3. 03

        Overlap every viewAim for 60–80% shared content while moving around the object.

      4. 04

        Capture high and low anglesShow the top, sides, underside edges, and occluded areas.

      5. 05

        Hold focus and zoom stableReject blur; never digitally resize the source photos.

      6. 06

        Give tracking some textureA matte object and feature-rich background register better.

      7. 07

        Avoid transparent or reflective partsThey violate ordinary photogrammetry assumptions.

      8. 08

        Move slowly for videoCircle the object once without abrupt jumps or motion blur.

      9. 09

        Add scale when dimensions matterMeasure two visible reconstructed points accurately.

      10. 10

        Verify critical geometryNever manufacture from an uninspected inferred surface.
