CAPTURE CONTRACT / 03

Better coverage makes
better reconstruction.

  1. 01

    Show the whole objectLeave space around every edge and use a plain, contrasting background.

  2. 02

    Keep the camera fixedFor an orbit or video, rotate the object—not the camera—and keep zoom and lighting unchanged.

  3. 03

    Cover one exact revolutionOrder 20–50 evenly spaced photos, or record one steady constant-speed turn.

  4. 04

    Add one real measurementMeasure the object’s maximum horizontal width; verify every critical feature before manufacturing.
